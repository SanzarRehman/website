FAQ

The questions that come up first.

Who is the platform for?

Organizations that handle vehicle titles at volume tow and recovery operators working abandoned vehicles, fleets, retailers, lenders, and insurers on the transaction types the state clearinghouse certifies.

How does pricing work?

A per-title service fee, charged only when the title issues. Government fees, clearinghouse fees, and the service fee are itemized separately, and government fees are collected on behalf of the state and passed through without markup.

What is checked before a packet reaches the state?

Checks that target the most common causes of rejection: signatures present on every document, title and application addresses that match, odometer disclosure where it is required, and legible images. Submission stays locked until every document is confirmed and every check has passed.

What happens when the state rejects a submission?

The clearinghouse returns the examiner's reason as free text. ConveyPath translates it into a plain-language guided fix pointing at the affected document; you replace that document and resubmit on the same order. Nothing starts over, and every prior version remains in the audit history.

Do you support electronic signatures?

Yes. Secure electronic Power of Attorney is supported through the clearinghouse's SE-POA service, which meets federal e-sign standards and works with any E-SIGN and UETA compliant signature provider. You can also bring a POA executed in any jurisdiction.

How are statutory waiting periods handled?

As a first-class status rather than silence: the case shows days remaining, the clear date, and a plain statement that no action is needed, so long-running work never reads as stalled.
