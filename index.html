What buyers ask first.

What parts of the title workflow can Conveyexa support?

Intake and evidence extraction, guided completion of the application, pre-submission validation, submission, status tracking and the handling of anything sent back. Which transaction types are in scope is agreed during implementation.

Can Conveyexa integrate with our existing systems?

Yes. The platform exposes REST APIs and connects to identity, document, notification and reporting systems. Integration scope is defined with your team, and Conveyexa works from your systems of record rather than replacing them.

Does Conveyexa replace our current platform?

Not necessarily. Many organizations run Conveyexa as the title-operations layer beside the systems they already use. Where a legacy tool is retired, that is a decision made during scoping, not a precondition.

How configurable are workflows and jurisdiction-specific rules?

Workflows are driven by transaction type and jurisdiction. Required steps, documents and checks are configured per type, and rules are versioned so a change never silently alters a transaction already in flight.

How does Conveyexa reduce manual data entry?

Uploaded evidence is read and used to pre-populate the application. Each value carries a pointer to the page it came from, so users verify rather than retype, and anything the reading was unsure about is flagged for a look.

How does Conveyexa help prevent avoidable rejections?

Checks run before submission against the data, the documents and the requirements that apply. Each problem names what is wrong and points at the field or document that fixes it, and submission stays locked until every check passes.

What does implementation typically involve?

Scoping the transaction types and jurisdictions in play, configuring workflows and rules, connecting the integrations you need, onboarding users and roles, and a supervised first run. Timelines depend on scope and are agreed up front.

How is pricing structured?

Pricing is structured per transaction, with platform and integration terms scoped to your volume. Government fees are itemized separately and passed through. Commercial terms are confirmed during scoping.
